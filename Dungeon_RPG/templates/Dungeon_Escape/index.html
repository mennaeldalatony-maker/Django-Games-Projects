📜 How to Play

🎯 Game Objective

Navigate through 5 Dungeon Rooms, defeat monsters, collect gold, and survive to escape!

🕹️ Action Controls
  • Attack: Deal 15-30 damage to the monster. The monster counters with a strike!
  • Heal: Spend 15 Gold to restore 25-40 HP.
  • Flee Room: 50% chance to jump to the next room without fighting.
🏆 How to Win

Reach Room 5 (Boss Room) and defeat the Boss. Fleeing is disabled in the final room!
