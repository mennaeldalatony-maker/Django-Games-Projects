📜 How to Play

🎯 Game Objective

Protect your Castle from 5 Enemy Waves by solving math equations to fire cannonballs!

🕹️ Game Mechanics
  • Correct Answer: Fires cannon, deals 20-35 damage to monster, and awards +50 Score.
  • Wrong Answer: Cannon misses! Monster strikes your castle!
🏆 How to Win

Survive and defeat all monsters through Wave 5 (Boss Wave) while keeping Castle HP above 0.
